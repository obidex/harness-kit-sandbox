# Harness Kit · owner defaults

> Loaded in every session, after `core.md`. How this owner's agents work with him, in every
> project. Records (applies when, outcome, source, check): `.harness/catalogue/owner-defaults.md`.
> A project may only tighten these, through an exception in its profile that names a decision.

- **O01 Act within authorization.** Do the work yourself. Ask the owner only for what stays
  reserved or unavailable: creating an account, token or app; a payment; a physical action or a
  step on his machine; a secret; a paid or owner-only setting; a gate or design approval; a refused
  action; a decision only he can make.
- **O02 One batch.** Everything needed from him goes in ONE `WAITING FOR YOU` message, at most a
  few steps, tedious part first. Each step is exact to the click and keystroke: the link, the
  button names, the text to paste, what he sees when it worked. Never "configure X". What a session
  can detect, he never confirms. Relative times only. A new batch replaces the open one.
- **O03 Decisions.** A decision comes as A/B (at most C), a one-line trade-off each and the
  recommendation; he answers with a letter. Inside an approved plan, take the recommended option
  yourself and report it.
- **O04 Secrets.** Never ask him to paste a secret into chat; secrets go into GitHub or the tool
  itself. Verify a secret by its name, never by reading it. A secret seen outside its store is
  burned: report it for rotation.
- **O05 His approval is his own act.** No agent posts, reviews, reacts or merges standing for the
  owner's approval.
- **O06 Model roles.** Sonnet for clear, bounded work; Opus for hard or risky work (ambiguous
  rules, cross-module design, hard diagnosis, money, permissions, data integrity, recovery). Set at
  the start of a thread, never switched mid-thread; good work is never redone for its model.
- **O07 Verification by risk.** Independent review scales with risk (C10), Opus preferred for
  tier 3. Optional reviewers are requested once, never waited on and never an availability
  dependency; each finding they deliver ends FIXED, TRACKED or DISMISSED with evidence.
- **O08 Short headline reports.** Lead with the status word and the answer; terse, plain
  language, decision-first; no narrated process.
- **O09 No Claude attribution.** Commits are authored as the owner; squash bodies carry no
  `Co-authored-by`; no "Generated by" footers in commits, PRs, issues or comments.
- **O10 Alerts.** Telegram only for unresolved failures and action-required events, deduplicated;
  never for success or progress.
- **O11 The owner is not a merge gate.** He decides and may veto; threads merge their own PRs on
  the gates (C12).
- **O12 Read for meaning.** He types fast. When two readings lead to different irreversible
  actions, ask one yes/no in the batch; otherwise act on the meaning.
