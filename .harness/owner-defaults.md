# Harness Kit · owner defaults

> After `core.md`. Records: `.harness/catalogue/owner-defaults.md`. Projects only tighten these,
> by a profile exception naming a decision.

- **O01 Act within authorization.** Do the work yourself. Ask the owner only for what stays
  reserved: an account, token or app; a payment; a physical or on-his-machine step; a secret; a
  paid or owner-only setting; a gate or design approval; a refused action; his decision.
- **O02 One batch.** Everything needed from him goes in ONE `WAITING FOR YOU` message, at most a few
  steps, tedious part first. Each step is exact: the link, the button, the text to paste, what he
  sees on success. Never "configure X". What a session can detect, he never confirms. Relative
  times only. A new batch replaces the open one.
- **O03 Decisions.** A decision comes as A/B (at most C), a one-line trade-off each and the
  recommendation; he answers with a letter. Inside an approved plan, take the recommendation
  and report it.
- **O04 Secrets.** Secrets go into GitHub or the tool, never into chat. Check a secret by
  name, never by reading it. A secret seen outside its store is burned: report it for rotation.
- **O05 His approval is his own act.** No agent posts, reviews, reacts or merges as his approval.
- **O06 Model roles.** Plain status: existing checks, no extra turn. Haiku at low effort
  reads logs and summarizes, only when it saves real work; never writes money, stock or permission
  code, never reviews. Building: the smallest reliable model (Sonnet for clear, bounded work);
  Opus for architecture, accounting, security, hard or risky work. Set per thread, never
  switched; good work is not redone for its model.
- **O07 Verification by risk.** Independent review scales with risk (C10), Opus preferred for tier
  3. Optional reviewers: asked once, never waited on; each finding ends FIXED, TRACKED
  or DISMISSED with evidence.
- **O08 Short headline reports.** Status word and answer first; terse, plain; no narrated process.
- **O09 No Claude attribution.** Commits authored as the owner; no `Co-authored-by` in
  squash bodies; no "Generated by" text anywhere.
- **O10 Alerts.** One bot, one Telegram group, every sender through `notify.mjs`
  (`.harness/alerts.md`): "Needs you" only for owner action or outages; quiet 23-08 Damascus;
  every PROBLEM ends RESOLVED; never success.
- **O11 The owner is not a merge gate.** He may veto; threads merge their PRs on the gates
  (C12).
- **O12 Read for meaning.** If two readings lead to different irreversible actions, ask one yes/no
  in the batch; else act on the meaning.
- **O13 Metered resources need notice.** Hosted minutes, paid services, storage or other metered
  use grow only after the owner hears what and an estimate. Schedules run no more often than needed
  (event or daily beats hourly); name unmetered minutes.
- **O14 Cross-project work goes through the inbox.** Work for another project is an `inbox` issue
  in its repo (`inbox` skill) naming the owner decision or delegation covering it; it proceeds only
  once that cover is verified in scope (citing never expands it). State and evidence stay there.
- **O15 Never a relay.** No automation, routine or thread asks the owner to wake, nudge or relay
  between sessions (O18's "check" aside); what cannot reach one records state for the next scheduled
  check: at most a daily-summary line, never "Needs you".
- **O16 Close threads.** A thread is resolved in the turn it reports DONE; anything due later is a
  coordinator's scheduled check-in, never an idle thread.
- **O17 Owner effort.** Before an owner step goes out, try in order an API or token, a pre-filled
  link, a guided script, then bundling; send the rest with its action count and minutes.
- **O18 Outside checkers.** A checker no session can wake is no wait point: the independent reviewer
  runs its checklist (checksums rebuilt, owner-effort pass, no secret on the wrong machine), the step
  goes out, the checker audits after. Only spending or production waits for it; his message
  says: tell <checker> "check".
